Welcome!

I'm UM. I am an actress, singer, and writer.

On this website, I'll share a bit about myself, and elaborate on my creative passions, specifically theatre and music.

About me:

I've had a passion for storytelling since childhood. I started off writing fictional stories, and then shifted to poetry, and finally, lyrics and music. I did my first musical theatre production at 6 years old, and fell in love with performance. Since then, I've trained in stage and screen acting, vocal performance, and five genres of dance.
Outside of my creative endeavors, I am interested in religion and its impact on world view, as well as music as a form of worship in varying cultures.
