About Me

I am an actress, singer, and writer. I've been doing theatre productions since I was about 6 years old, and I write music. I'm currently working on releasing my first single, "Distance." I'm considering majoring in either film or acting in college. I am also extremely interested in various religions and their impacts on world views, as well as the role of music in worship around the world. I love spending time in nature and playing board games with my family, especially Bananagrams. My favorite colors right now are blue and green, and my favorite animal is a hawk. In terms of computer science, this class was my first encounter with coding, and I learned some very valuable skills that I believe will be useful in terms of critical thinking, problem solving, and technological navigation.
