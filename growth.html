Unit 6 Project: Hawaii Landscape

This work represents an area of growth for me because I had a lot of difficulty with this project, and got stuck half way through, but I problem-solved, and by the end, I fully understood the content. It was most difficult to understand and master the if-else-if statements for this project. I got stuck because I didn’t understand how to code my if-else-if statement, but I continued to try different things and look back at the instructions until I figured out my mistake and was able to fix it. I put a lot of effort into this project in order to problem-solve the things that I originally couldn’t do, and by the end, I felt like I had mastered the content. Knowing what I know now, I would try to fix the directions that the birds are flying in, and try to make the trees more consistent in proportions.
